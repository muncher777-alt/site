Online prizedraw fundraiser for Gaza

Our aim: to raise as much as possible to keep food reaching families on the ground in Gaza. This is a joint fundraiser between Asafeer and the Gaza Food Relief Fund.

The prizes

  • Free lunch for two from @edamibakery
  • Handmade Tatreez embroidered thobe and bag by @thobna
  • Handmade Tatreez by @wanaspace
  • Pottery class
  • Pasta making class
  • "I love Falasteen" T-shirts
  • "I love Falasteen" tank top

The cause

Funds go to the on-the-ground Gaza food relief project feeding families who need it most right now, and to selected, vetted families in Gaza who receive direct support through Asafeer.

How to enter

Donate any amount to get an entry, or follow the free entry guidelines on the prizedraw page. Make sure to include your email address, Instagram account and home address. Winners are announced by email and Instagram.
