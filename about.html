ABOUT US

About Asafeer

Asafeer began with a simple, direct connection: real conversations with real families in Gaza, living through a humanitarian crisis that has taken everything from them but their will to survive.

We're a small UK charity, run by trustees who give their time for free, built on the belief that aid works best when it moves quickly, honestly and directly, from the people who care to the people who need it.

What we do

We provide direct financial and material assistance to individuals and families affected by humanitarian crisis in Gaza, and, where the need is greatest, across the wider region. That means help with the essentials: food, clean water, shelter, medical supplies, fuel, and the other necessities of life that a crisis takes away first.

We raise what we give through community fundraising here in London, with music nights, poetry evenings and cultural events, alongside online appeals and individual donations. Every pound is accounted for, and every beneficiary is verified before a single payment is made.

Aid works best when it moves quickly, honestly and directly.

HOW WE WORK

Trust is everything

Before any family receives support, we confirm who they are and where they are through direct contact: a real conversation, a live video call, careful checks. The people behind every donation deserve to know it reaches exactly who it's meant for.

Verified

Every family is verified personally before a single payment is made.

Approved together

Every payment is approved by more than one trustee, and every transfer is recorded.

Reviewed

Every quarter, our full board reviews how funds have moved.

Who we are

Asafeer is led by a small board of trustees based in London, working entirely as volunteers. We are currently completing our registration with the Charity Commission for England and Wales.

We hold ourselves to this standard because the families we support deserve nothing less, and because the people who trust us with their donations deserve to know where every penny goes.

Get involved

Whether it's coming to an event, sharing what we do, or making a donation, every bit of support gets us closer to another family having what they need to get through another day.
