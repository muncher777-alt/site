We raise funds for families in Gaza, Palestine and Lebanon.

Run by Palestinian, Italian and Welsh trustees in London, with a verified network of contacts and direct relationships with families on the ground.

London Gaza · Palestine · Lebanon

Every donation goes straight to the family, with no middleman deciding on their behalf. They choose how to use it. Research on humanitarian cash transfers consistently shows that giving people direct, unconditional support helps them meet their most urgent needs and plan further ahead, because they understand their own circumstances better than anyone deciding for them ever could.

A FAMILY WE SUPPORT

Noor's story

Noor is a mother of three, displaced from her home in Gaza after it was destroyed. She and her children now live in a tent camp.

We've been in direct contact with Noor since the start, verifying her family, understanding what they need, and staying in touch as things change. Thanks to donations through Asafeer, we've already been able to provide Noor's family with groceries to get them through difficult weeks.

We send regular updates to donors showing exactly what their donations went toward.
