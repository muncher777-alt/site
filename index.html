HOW WE STARTED

Real conversations, not a system

Asafeer began with a simple, direct connection: real conversations with real families in Gaza, living through a humanitarian crisis that has taken everything from them but their will to survive.

We're a small UK charity, run entirely by volunteer trustees, built on the belief that aid works best when it moves quickly, honestly, and directly — from the people who care, to the people who need it.

Before any family receives support, we confirm who they are and where they are through direct contact: a real conversation, a live video call, careful checks. Every payment is approved by more than one trustee, every transfer is recorded, and every quarter our board reviews how funds have moved.

"Direct funds, real choice — families decide what they need, not us."

HOW IT WORKS

Three steps, every time

No shortcuts, no exceptions — the same process for every family we help.

1

Verify

We confirm who someone is and where they are — ID, a live call, a real conversation — before a penny moves.

2

Approve

Every payment is signed off by more than one trustee. No single person decides alone.

3

Deliver

Funds go straight to the family through traceable channels, and we follow up to know they arrived.
