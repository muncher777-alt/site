We raise funds for families in Gaza, Palestine and Lebanon.

We personally verify every family's ID, stay in direct contact with them, and understand exactly what they need, then raise the funds here in the UK to get it to them, verified every step of the way.

Donate now → Ways to give

Run by Palestinian, Italian and Welsh trustees in London, with a verified network of contacts and direct relationships with families on the ground.

London Gaza · Palestine · Lebanon

Every donation goes straight to the family, with no organisation in between and no middleman deciding on their behalf. They choose how to use it. Research on humanitarian cash transfers consistently shows that giving people direct, unconditional support helps them meet their most urgent needs and plan further ahead, because they understand their own circumstances better than anyone deciding for them ever could.

Noor's story

Noor is a mother of three, displaced from her home in Gaza after it was destroyed. She and her children now live in a tent camp.
