The families we support

Every family is verified by us personally, and we stay in direct contact with them. Here are the families we are raising funds for.

Noor's story

Noor is a mother of three, displaced from her home in Gaza after it was destroyed. She and her children now live in a tent camp.

We've been in direct contact with Noor since the start, verifying her family, understanding what they need, and staying in touch as things change. Thanks to donations through Asafeer, we've already been able to provide Noor's family with groceries to get them through difficult weeks.

We send regular updates to donors showing exactly what their donations went toward.
